Privacy notice

How VisiTrack handles your visitor information.

Effective date: [date]

In short

VisiTrack collects your visitor details only to keep audit reports and documents of who visits PLM. These records can be seen only by authorized administrators.

1 Who we are

VisiTrack is the visitor management system of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Maynila. PLM is the personal information controller responsible for the data you give us. This notice follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

2 What we collect

When you pre-register

Full name, mobile number, email address (optional), company or organization (optional), the person and department you are visiting, purpose of visit, date and arrival time, and number of visitors in your group. We also create a reference number for your visit.

When you arrive at the gate

Your reference number, the time you enter and leave, the storage slot assigned to your belongings or ID, and whether your request was accepted or rejected.

3 Why we collect it

We use your details only for:

  • Audit reports of visitors to PLM; and
  • Official documents and records of visits.

We do not use your information for marketing, and we do not sell it.

4 Our basis for processing

We process your information with your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box on the pre-registration form. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer, though we may still need to keep records of a visit that already happened.

5 Who can see it

Visitor records and reports can be seen only by authorized VisiTrack administrators. Security personnel at the gate see only what they need to check you in and out, such as your name, reference number, and the office you are visiting. Staff accounts are protected by log-in, and each person can only reach the screens their role allows.

We do not share your data with outside parties, unless the law or a lawful order requires it.

6 How long we keep it

We keep visit records for [retention period, e.g. 1 year] to support audits and documentation. After that, they are securely deleted or anonymized.

7 How we protect it

We limit access by role, require log-in for staff, use secure connections, and keep a record of who accesses the system. Only the minimum information needed is collected.

8 Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used;
  • Access the data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent;
  • Request that your data be erased or blocked, where allowed;
  • Receive a copy of your data in a usable format; and
  • Claim damages if your data is misused.

If you believe your rights were violated, you may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) .

9 Visitors under 18

A parent or legal guardian must give consent for visitors below 18 years old.
